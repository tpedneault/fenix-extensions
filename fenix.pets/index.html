Install

  1. In Fenix, press SPC h x to open the Extensions page.
  2. Search for Pets, and install it. Fenix shows what it may do before it installs anything.

Or download the package and run fenix --install-extension fenix.pets-1.3.0.fenixext.

What it may do

Fenix holds it to these, and shows them before installing it. Everything else is closed to it.

Draws over the editor

About

Little 3D pets that live on Fenix's modeline: Ember (Fenix's phoenix), Ferris the crab, a cat, a rubber duck, an owl, a fox and a snail. They're low-poly toys, drawn toon-shaded and outlined in your theme's light -- chunky pixel style by default, or smooth.

They wander, sit, look about and stretch; hop up onto the edges of your panes; and nap together when you've stopped typing for a while. Now and then one comes up to sit in the margin beside the line you're on, and follows you from line to line, or trots over to where you're working. Each has its ways: the owl hoots, turning its head right round, and is up when you're up late; the fox creeps up on something in the grass and leaps; the snail takes its time, and hides right inside its shell; the cat bats its yarn about and pounces on it.

They notice what you do:

  • Save and the one nearest the caret hops.

  • Errors in the file send them into hiding until they're fixed.

  • A passing task and they all celebrate.

  • A failing task: Ember bursts into flame and burns down to ash, and rises again the next time a task passes. The rubber duck comes over to listen. The cat sits it out.

  • Type for a while without stopping and Ember catches fire, running along the modeline trailing flames.

  • Debug and the rubber duck comes to sit by the line you're on and listens until the session's over. It quacks at each breakpoint, and the others start.

  • Come back after a while away and they look up and greet you.

  • Open another file and the one nearest the caret looks round.

  • Click one for a heart.

  • They play together, too: the cat stalks the duck, and they chase each other, race, and get the zoomies when they're happy. In the snow they gather round Ember to keep warm.

  • New warnings get a tilt of the head from the one nearest the caret.

  • Late at night they nap after a minute without typing -- all but the owl.

  • With the Pomodoro extension, they keep still and quiet in a work round, and stretch and play when the break comes.

  • Commit and the one nearest the caret throws confetti; push and they hop to see it off; a merge conflict sends them into hiding until it's resolved.

  • A task starts and one sits down to watch it, dozing off if it drags. 100 tests or more passed is a party.

  • A coding agent finishes and one cheers; an error, and Ember singes. They hear only that it finished, never what it said.

Looking after them

Saving, passing tests, petting and playing fetch teach them things. Each level unlocks something to wear: a bow at level 2, a scarf at 3, sunglasses at 4, a party hat at 5, a crown at 7. Happiness drifts down a little while nobody plays with them, never below content, and never while you're away: a day off costs nothing.

In December they wear Santa hats and it snows; for Halloween, pumpkins; on New Year's Day, and on the day you adopted each of them (a year or more on), party hats.

KeyWhat it does
SPC P aAdopt a pet
SPC P bThrow the ball: it bounces along, and the nearest pet fetches it back to the caret
SPC P pTheir home: levels, happiness, what they wear; pet, dress, rename or rehome one
SPC P wDress one up
SPC P hHide them, or bring them back
SPC P rRehome one

With standard keys they're Ctrl+K P a and so on. What they say names the keys as the key scheme in use has them, and yours when you've bound your own: "C-k P a adopts one" with standard keys, "SPC P a" with Vim keys. It needs Fenix 1.5 or later.

Setting
pets.placemodeline, or the foot of the pane you're in (panes)
pets.sizesmall, medium or large
pets.stylepixel or smooth
pets.mostHow many you may have at once
pets.livelinesscalm, normal or lively
pets.visitsWhether one comes up to sit beside your line now and then
pets.seasonsSnow and seasonal hats

They fade while they pass the line you're on and never take a key. With Fenix's animations off they stand still.

Making them

The models are built in code, from simple shapes jointed like toys: src/modeler (cargo run --release there) writes models/*.glb. The effects are pixel art: python src/fx.py writes sprites/fx.png. To see the models as Fenix draws them, every clip, use Fenix's model preview (FENIX_MODEL_PREVIEW=models cargo test -p fenix-gui preview -- --ignored in Fenix).

Changelog

1.3.0

  • For Fenix 1.5 (extension API 1.5): what the pets say names the keys of the key scheme in use, and yours when you've bound your own -- "C-k P a adopts one" with standard keys, SPC P a with Vim keys, and likewise hiding them, rehoming one and, at a new level, their home. The README gives standard keys beside Vim's.

1.2.0

  • pets.home can be run from outside Fenix, over the control channel (fenix/runCommand): its page opened by a script or another tool, as the screenshots' scripts do.

1.1.0

For Fenix 1.0.1 (extension API 1.1).

  • New friends: an owl that's up when you're up late and turns its head right round to hoot, a fox that creeps up on something in the grass and leaps on it, and a snail in no hurry that hides right inside its shell.

  • The cat has a ball of yarn: it bats it, creeps after it, and pounces.

  • Rubber duck debugging: while you debug, the rubber duck sits by the line you're on and listens. It quacks at each breakpoint, the others start, and it's pleased when the session ends.

  • They notice more: back after a while away, they look up and greet you; another file gets a look round from the one nearest the caret; new warnings get a tilt of the head.

  • Livelier: they walk more, linger less, play together more often, get the zoomies when they're happy, race each other, and now and then the one nearest the caret trots over to where you're working. In the snow, they gather round Ember to keep warm.

  • How busy they are is up to you: pets.liveliness, calm, normal or lively.

  • Late at night (22:00 to 06:00) they nap after a minute without typing, not three -- all but the owl.

  • With the Pomodoro extension (1.0.1), they keep still and quiet in a work round, and stretch and play when the break comes.

  • They draw only when something's changed: an unchanged layer isn't sent again, so Fenix doesn't redraw five times a second for pets that are asleep.

  • They move as smoothly as their style asks: Fenix 1.0.0 drew a model's next frame only when something else redrew.

  • Commits get confetti from the pet nearest the caret. A push and they all hop to see it off; a pull, or another branch, and they look round. A merge conflict sends them into hiding until it's resolved, and then they cheer.

  • A big test run is a party: 100 or more tests passed and none failed, and there's confetti from every one of them.

  • A build or a test run starting gets a watcher: the pet nearest the caret sits down to watch, and dozes off if it drags on past two minutes.

  • A coding agent finishing its turn gets a cheer; an error, and Ember singes and the others look round. They're told only that a turn ended and how, never what was said.

1.0.0
