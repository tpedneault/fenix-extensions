Install

  1. In Fenix, press SPC h x to open the Extensions page.
  2. Search for Jira, and install it. Fenix shows what it may do before it installs anything.

Or download the package and run fenix --install-extension fenix.jira-1.1.0.fenixext.

What it may do

Fenix holds it to these, and shows them before installing it. Everything else is closed to it.

Makes web (HTTP) requests to these hosts
  • ${setting:jira.base_url} those the setting jira.base_url holds
Sees where you are in the editor – the file, the cursor, the selection – when its commands run
Reads and keeps secrets in Fenix's secret store, under these keys
  • jira.token

About

Your Jira Server or Data Center in Fenix. Its page (SPC j j) lists your searches on the left -- assigned to you, reported by you, watching, recently updated, each tracked project's open issues and sprint, each tracked person's, and your saved JQL -- with the selected search's issues in the middle and a preview on the right.

SPC j g opens an issue by key, SPC j / searches (words, or JQL after :), and SPC j n makes an issue with the project's real types and fields.

An issue's keys

KeyWhat it does
sMove it through its workflow
p A uPriority, assignee, due date
e ETitle, description
CComment
TLog time
a tAdd it to the agenda (and start the clock)
y oCopy its link, open it in the browser

Setting it up

Its server is jira.base_url and its token jira.token, both on the settings page (SPC ,); FENIX_JIRA_TOKEN overrides the token. The token is a personal access token, sent as a bearer token.

SettingWhat it does
jira.projectsProjects whose open issues and sprint are listed
jira.usersPeople whose issues are listed
jira.queriesSaved searches
jira.blockedPer project, what Blocked means: a status, the Flagged field, or nothing
jira.prioritiesHow Jira's priorities map onto the agenda's
jira.sync_minutesHow often the agenda's linked tasks are brought up to date

For the agenda

The agenda extension reaches Jira through this one's services: jira.request, jira.config, jira.set_blocked and jira.new_issue.
