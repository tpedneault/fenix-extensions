Install

  1. In Fenix, press SPC h x to open the Extensions page, and its Bundles tab.
  2. Search for Mission, and install it. Fenix shows what it may do before it installs anything.

Or download the package and run fenix --install-extension fenix.mission-bundle-1.0.0.fenixext.

What it may do

Nothing of its own: a bundle runs no code. Each extension it installs asks for its own permissions, below, and Fenix shows them before installing.

What it installs

  • CCSDS & ECSS fenix.ccsds ^1

    Space packets, transfer frames and CLTUs decoded through the MIB, recordings and live telemetry with a parameter followed through them, on-board times, hex, and the CCSDS and ECSS standards

    May use: network connections, project files, folders of yours, the editor, other settings, the clipboard

  • SCOS-2000 MIB fenix.mib ^1

    SCOS-2000 MIBs: browse them, telecommands and parameters in your scripts (K, gd, completion, checks), the telecommand insert form with repeated groups and value sets, command sequences, two MIBs compared, and XTCE, C, Python or a Wireshark dissector generated from them

    May use: project files, folders of yours, the editor, other settings

  • Tcl fenix.tcl ^1

    Tcl scripts: Tcl's own usage lines on K, completion of options, subcommands, built-ins and your procs, go to definition, and the project's definitions by name

    May use: runs programs, project files, the editor

About

Mission work: SCOS-2000 MIBs, CCSDS and PUS packets decoded through them, and Tcl scripts. One install on the welcome (SPC h w) or the Extensions page's Bundles (SPC h x) brings them all.

ExtensionWhat
fenix.mibSCOS-2000 MIBs, and their telecommands and parameters in your scripts
fenix.ccsdsSpace packets, frames and CLTUs, recordings and live telemetry
fenix.tclTcl scripts: usage on K, completion, go to definition

Removing the bundle removes them too, unless you installed one on its own or another extension needs it.
