# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Rubber duck debugging: while you debug, the rubber duck sits by the line you're on and listens. It quacks at each breakpoint, the others start, and it's pleased when the session ends.
- Back after a while away, they look up and greet you. Moving to another file, the one nearest the caret looks round.
- Livelier: they walk more and sit and idle for less time, play together more often, and get the zoomies when they're happy.
- Their animation is as smooth as their style asks for, with Fenix 1.0.1: Fenix 1.0.0 drew a model's next frame only when something else redrew.

## 1.0.0

- The first release, for Fenix 1.0: little 3D pets on the modeline: they nap, play fetch, cheer your passing tests -- and Ember burns up when one fails.
