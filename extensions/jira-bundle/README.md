# Jira

Your Jira Server or Data Center, and the agenda kept in step with it.
One install on the welcome (`SPC h w`) or the Extensions page's Bundles
(`SPC h x`) brings them all.

| Extension | What |
|---|---|
| `fenix.jira` | Searches, issues and their workflow, a new-issue form |
| `fenix.agenda` | Tasks on a board, linked to Jira issues |

Removing the bundle removes them too, unless you installed one on its
own or another extension needs it.
