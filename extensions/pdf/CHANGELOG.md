# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- The modeline no longer shows a PDF's page on other files when the PDF came back with the session, in a tab behind them.
- `PDF opening...` leaves the modeline when a PDF can't be opened.

## 1.0.0

- The first release, for Fenix 1.0: pDFs read in a pane: continuous pages, zoom, an outline, search, selection, links and marks, and a shelf of the documents you read.
