# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- For Fenix 1.5 (extension API 1.5): what it says names the keys of the
  key scheme in use, and yours where you rebound them -- Home's
  **reading** shows the shelf's keys, `C-k r f` with standard keys and
  `SPC r f` with Vim keys, and shows them again when you switch; "no PDF
  open" says the same keys, and an empty shelf points to the settings
  page by its keys (`C-,` or `SPC ,`).

## 1.0.1

- The modeline no longer shows a PDF's page on other files when the PDF came back with the session, in a tab behind them.
- `PDF opening...` leaves the modeline when a PDF can't be opened.

## 1.0.0

- The first release, for Fenix 1.0: pDFs read in a pane: continuous pages, zoom, an outline, search, selection, links and marks, and a shelf of the documents you read.
