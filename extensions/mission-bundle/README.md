# Mission

Mission work: SCOS-2000 MIBs, CCSDS and PUS packets decoded through
them, and Tcl scripts. One install on the welcome (`SPC h w`) or the
Extensions page's Bundles (`SPC h x`) brings them all.

| Extension | What |
|---|---|
| `fenix.mib` | SCOS-2000 MIBs, and their telecommands and parameters in your scripts |
| `fenix.ccsds` | Space packets, frames and CLTUs, recordings and live telemetry |
| `fenix.tcl` | Tcl scripts: usage on K, completion, go to definition |

Removing the bundle removes them too, unless you installed one on its
own or another extension needs it.
