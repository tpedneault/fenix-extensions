# Personal

Your notes and journal, Mermaid diagrams drawn as you type, and a board
of tasks with a clock for your time. One install on the welcome (`SPC h
w`) or the Extensions page's Bundles (`SPC h x`) brings them all.

| Extension | What |
|---|---|
| `fenix.notebook` | Notes, a daily journal, links and quick captures |
| `fenix.diagrams` | Mermaid diagrams drawn as you type |
| `fenix.agenda` | Tasks on a board, a clock for your time, a week's timesheet |

Removing the bundle removes them too, unless you installed one on its
own or another extension needs it.
