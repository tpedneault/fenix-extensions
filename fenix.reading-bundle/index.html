Install

  1. In Fenix, press SPC h x to open the Extensions page, and its Bundles tab.
  2. Search for Reading, and install it. Fenix shows what it may do before it installs anything.

Or download the package and run fenix --install-extension fenix.reading-bundle-1.0.0.fenixext.

What it may do

Nothing of its own: a bundle runs no code. Each extension it installs asks for its own permissions, below, and Fenix shows them before installing.

What it installs

  • PDF reader fenix.pdf ^1

    PDFs read in a pane: continuous pages, zoom, an outline, search, selection, links and marks, and a shelf of the documents you read

    May use: runs programs, the editor

About

PDFs read in a pane, with an outline, search, marks and a shelf of what you read. One install on the welcome (SPC h w) or the Extensions page's Bundles (SPC h x) brings them all.

ExtensionWhat
fenix.pdfPDFs read in a pane

Removing the bundle removes them too, unless you installed one on its own or another extension needs it.
