Install

  1. In Fenix, press SPC h x to open the Extensions page.
  2. Search for Pomodoro, and install it. Fenix shows what it may do before it installs anything.

Or download the package and run fenix --install-extension fenix.pomodoro-1.2.0.fenixext.

What it may do

Fenix holds it to these, and shows them before installing it. Everything else is closed to it.

Plays sounds of its own

About

A focus timer for Fenix: a work round, then a break, and a long break every few rounds. The round counts down on the modeline -- 🍅 18:42 while you work, ☕ 04:12 on a break -- and when it's over Fenix chimes and a notification says what comes next: Enter on it, or a click, starts it. The next round's end replaces it, and starting a round puts it away. A round you skip is said on the message line.

SPC o p s starts a round. A break starts by itself when the work before it ends (pomodoro.auto_breaks); the next work round waits for you. Rounds are kept against the clock, so one carries on while Fenix is closed, and one that ran out meanwhile is counted.

KeyWhat it does
SPC o p sStart the round, or go on with a paused one
SPC o p pPause it
SPC o p nSkip to the next round (a skipped work round isn't counted)
SPC o p rBack to the first round
SPC o p oThe page: the round, today's rounds, the week's
SPC o p tThe agenda task work rounds clock into

With standard keys they're Ctrl+K o p s and so on. What it says names the keys as the key scheme in use has them, and yours when you've bound your own: "C-k o p s starts it" with standard keys, "SPC o p s" with Vim keys. It needs Fenix 1.5 or later.

The page

The round under way and how far it's gone, what comes after it, today's work rounds (when each ended, how long, the task), and the last seven days. Four weeks are kept, in pomodoro.json.

KeyWhat it does
sStart, or pause
nSkip
rReset
tPick the agenda task

With the agenda

With the Agenda extension, a work round runs the agenda's clock:

  • If its clock is already running, the round is for that task, and the clock is left as it is.

  • If it isn't, the round clocks into the task picked with SPC o p t (in progress or to do), and the clock stops when the round ends, is paused or reset.

Today's rounds on the page name the task each was for. pomodoro.agenda turns it off.

Settings

SettingWhat it does
pomodoro.workA work round, in minutes (25)
pomodoro.short_breakA short break (5)
pomodoro.long_breakA long break (15)
pomodoro.long_everyWork rounds to a long break (4)
pomodoro.auto_breaksWhether a break starts by itself when work ends
pomodoro.agendaWhether work rounds clock into the agenda
pomodoro.soundWhether a round that runs out chimes (on)
pomodoro.unfocusedWhether to warn when you wander off during work (off)

A setting changed mid-round applies from the next round.

The "unfocused" warning

With pomodoro.unfocused on, going to a page during a work round -- another extension's, Fenix's settings or Home -- says so on the message line, once a round: Round 2 has 18:42 to go -- this page isn't your work. Files are your work, whichever project they're in (it isn't told which file, only that it's one), and so are terminals, the explorer and its own page. A break, or a round paused, never warns.

What it may do

  • Play its own chime (sound = true), sounds/chime.wav: two sine tones, made for it.

  • Be told what has the focus (events = ["focus"]): a file, text, or a page and its kind -- not which file.

Changelog

1.2.0

For Fenix 1.5 (extension API 1.5):

  • A round that runs out is a notification, not a line in the message line: what comes next, and Enter on it, or a click, starts it. The next round's end replaces it, and starting a round puts it away. It still chimes (pomodoro.sound); a round you skip is still said on the message line.

  • What it says names the keys of the key scheme in use, and yours when you've bound your own: "C-k o p s starts it" with standard keys, SPC o p s with Vim keys, and the agenda's key to add a task.

  • The README gives standard keys beside Vim's.

1.1.0

  • pomodoro.open can be run from outside Fenix, over the control channel (fenix/runCommand): its page opened by a script or another tool, as the screenshots' scripts do.

1.0.1

  • pomodoro.state, a service other extensions read the timer through: {"phase": "work"}, "break" or "off". The pets keep still while you work and play in the break.

1.0.0

  • The first release, for Fenix 1.0: a focus timer: work rounds and breaks counted down on the modeline, today's rounds and the week's -- clocked into the agenda when it's there.
