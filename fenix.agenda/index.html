Install

  1. In Fenix, press SPC h x to open the Extensions page.
  2. Search for Agenda, and install it. Fenix shows what it may do before it installs anything.

Or download the package and run fenix --install-extension fenix.agenda-1.1.0.fenixext.

What it may do

Fenix holds it to these, and shows them before installing it. Everything else is closed to it.

Sees where you are in the editor – the file, the cursor, the selection – when its commands run

About

Your tasks on a board, a clock for your time, and a week's timesheet -- each task linked to a Jira issue when the Jira extension is there. Its keys are under SPC A, and your tasks are kept in agenda.json.

SPC A a opens it where it was left; SPC A b, SPC A l and SPC A r open the board, the list and this week's time. SPC A n adds a task, SPC A h adds one about the selection or line you're on, and SPC A t stops the clock, resumes it or switches it.

With standard keys they're Ctrl+K A a and so on. What it says names the keys as the key scheme in use has them, and yours when you've bound your own -- on the page, on Home's today and in its messages: "C-k A n to add one" with standard keys, "SPC A n" with Vim keys. It needs Fenix 1.5 or later.

With Jira

With the Jira extension installed and set up, a linked task's edits go to Jira, and Jira's come back every jira.sync_minutes:

  • SPC A i imports issues assigned to you

  • SPC A s syncs now

  • SPC A w reviews the week's unsent time and sends it as worklogs

A sync you asked for ends in a notification: how many issues it brought up to date, or why it failed. One that finds a conflict says so, even a timed one; Enter on it opens the agenda. Each replaces the last.

Settings

SettingWhat it does

agenda.idle_minutesWith the clock running, how long without a key before it asks what time to keep

Outside Fenix

The control channel's fenix/agenda.tasks, fenix/agenda.clockIn and fenix/agenda.clockOut, and the clock in fenix/state, are answered by this extension.

1.2.0

For Fenix 1.5 (extension API 1.5):

  • What it says names the keys of the key scheme in use, and yours when you've bound your own -- Home's today, the empty page's "C-k A i brings in your Jira issues" with standard keys (SPC A i with Vim keys), and its messages, the Jira page's and Fenix's settings' and extensions' keys among them.

  • A Jira sync ends in a notification, not the message line: how many issues it brought up to date, why it failed, or the conflicts it found, with the agenda a key away. A newer one replaces the last.

  • The README gives standard keys beside Vim's.
