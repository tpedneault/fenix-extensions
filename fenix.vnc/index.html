Install

  1. In Fenix, press SPC h x to open the Extensions page.
  2. Search for VNC, and install it. Fenix shows what it may do before it installs anything.

Or download the package and run fenix --install-extension fenix.vnc-1.0.0.fenixext.

What it may do

Fenix holds it to these, and shows them before installing it. Everything else is closed to it.

Opens network connections (TCP and UDP sockets) to these addresses
  • ${setting:vnc.hosts} those the setting vnc.hosts holds
  • *:5900 any host, this port
Keeps files in folders of yours
  • screenshots: the folder the setting vnc.screenshots names, else vnc-screenshots in Fenix's data folder
Reads the text on your clipboard

About

Remote desktops in a pane. List the machines you use in vnc.hosts, and SPC v v opens one -- a VM's console, a lab PC -- in the pane you're in. Each stays connected in the background once opened, shown or not, until you close it.

  • The keyboard: its pane takes every key, Caps Lock and the modifiers too (Ctrl-C, Alt-Tab and Ctrl-Alt-F2 go to the guest), until Ctrl+\ gives them back; a click takes them again.

  • The pointer: the guest's own, drawn by Fenix where the pointer is; its buttons and its wheel.

  • The clipboard: what the guest copies is on yours; yours goes to the guest when its pane takes the keyboard, so its own paste keys paste it.

  • Its size: a server that can be resized (ExtendedDesktopSize) is asked to be the pane's size, and asked again when the pane changes; one that can't is stretched to fill it.

  • A dropped connection says so on the pane and is dialled again, backing off (1, 2, 4, 8, 16 seconds), six times; then SPC v v on it tries again.

  • The bell is said in the message line.

It speaks RFB 3.3, 3.7 and 3.8, with no authentication or VNC Authentication (with an empty password), and the ZRLE, CopyRect and Raw encodings.

Keys

KeyCommandWhat it does
SPC v vvnc.openA picker over vnc.hosts: connect to one, or bring it here
SPC v qvnc.closeClose the session you're in
SPC v svnc.screenshotSave its picture as a PNG

With standard keys, SPC is Ctrl+K: Ctrl+K v v. vnc.open may run from outside Fenix too (fenix/runCommand). A [workspaces] entry vnc:NAME opens host NAME.

It needs Fenix 1.5 or later (extension API 1.5). What it says names the keys that run a command in the key scheme you use, and yours where you rebound them: a session that gave up says C-k v v reconnects with standard keys, SPC v v with Vim keys.

Settings

SettingWhat it is
vnc.hosts[[vnc.hosts]] tables of name, host and port (5900 when it's left out)
vnc.screenshotsWhere SPC v s saves pictures; empty keeps them in Fenix's data folder
 
[[vnc.hosts]]
name = "build-vm"
host = "10.0.0.5"

[[vnc.hosts]]
name = "test-vm"
host = "10.0.0.6"
port = 5901

No passwords: every host is taken to be on a trusted network.

What it may do

  • Open connections to the hosts in vnc.hosts, and to any host on port 5900.

  • Read your clipboard, when one of its panes takes the keyboard.

  • Write into its screenshots folder.
