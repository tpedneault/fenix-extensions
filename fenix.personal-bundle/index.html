Install

  1. In Fenix, press SPC h x to open the Extensions page, and its Bundles tab.
  2. Search for Personal, and install it. Fenix shows what it may do before it installs anything.

Or download the package and run fenix --install-extension fenix.personal-bundle-1.0.0.fenixext.

What it may do

Nothing of its own: a bundle runs no code. Each extension it installs asks for its own permissions, below, and Fenix shows them before installing.

What it installs

  • Agenda fenix.agenda ^1

    Tasks on a board, a clock for your time, and a week's timesheet -- linked to Jira issues when Jira is there

    May use: the editor

  • Diagrams fenix.diagrams ^1

    Mermaid diagrams drawn as you type, their errors on their lines, Fenix's themes and yours, and exports

    May use: project files, the editor, fonts

  • Notebook fenix.notebook ^1

    Notes, a daily journal, links and quick captures, kept for you in one folder -- an Obsidian vault works as it is

    May use: runs programs, project files, folders of yours, the editor

About

Your notes and journal, Mermaid diagrams drawn as you type, and a board of tasks with a clock for your time. One install on the welcome (SPC h w) or the Extensions page's Bundles (SPC h x) brings them all.

ExtensionWhat
fenix.notebookNotes, a daily journal, links and quick captures
fenix.diagramsMermaid diagrams drawn as you type

Removing the bundle removes them too, unless you installed one on its own or another extension needs it.
