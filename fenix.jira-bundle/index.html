Install

  1. In Fenix, press SPC h x to open the Extensions page, and its Bundles tab.
  2. Search for Jira, and install it. Fenix shows what it may do before it installs anything.

Or download the package and run fenix --install-extension fenix.jira-bundle-1.0.0.fenixext.

What it may do

Nothing of its own: a bundle runs no code. Each extension it installs asks for its own permissions, below, and Fenix shows them before installing.

What it installs

  • Agenda fenix.agenda ^1

    Tasks on a board, a clock for your time, and a week's timesheet -- linked to Jira issues when Jira is there

    May use: the editor

  • Jira fenix.jira ^1

    Your Jira Server or Data Center: searches, issues and their workflow, a new-issue form with the project's real fields -- and the agenda kept in step with it

    May use: web requests, the editor, secrets

About

Your Jira Server or Data Center, and the agenda kept in step with it. One install on the welcome (SPC h w) or the Extensions page's Bundles (SPC h x) brings them all.

ExtensionWhat
fenix.jiraSearches, issues and their workflow, a new-issue form

Removing the bundle removes them too, unless you installed one on its own or another extension needs it.
